# PsychLogic public website images

- `sample-note-1.png` and `sample-note-2.png`: included real note sample images.
- `sample-note-3.png` and `sample-note-4.png`: upload your chosen new note sample images using these exact filenames.
- To add sample 5 or later, upload an image to `assets/`, then add an entry with that filename to `SAMPLE_CATALOG` in `index.html`.
- `payment-qr.png`: upload your actual UPI payment QR image.

**Do not put customer payment screenshots or personal data into this public folder.** Buyers upload proof privately to your Drive through Apps Script.
